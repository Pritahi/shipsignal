# ShipSignal Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

ShipSignal ("the app") is a Forge app for Atlassian Jira built by prit Sindhav.

## What the app reads
When you install the app and run an evaluation, it reads Jira issue, version,
comment and project data from the site where it is installed, and Teamwork Graph
data (linked goals, pull requests, deployments, comments) for those issues,
using the permissions you grant at install time.

## What the app stores
The app keeps short-lived readiness snapshots in Atlassian-hosted Forge storage
(KVS) on your own site. No personal data is copied to systems outside Atlassian,
and nothing is sent to third-party servers. The app makes no external network
calls beyond Atlassian APIs.

## What the app never does
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- No sale or sharing of your data with anyone.
- No access to data outside the Jira site where the app is installed.

## Data removal
Uninstalling the app deletes its stored snapshots from your site's Forge
storage. Jira comments or issues the app created at your request remain under
your control and can be deleted like any other Jira content.
